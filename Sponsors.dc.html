Team Sponsorship

The students who make up The Grease Monkeys are pursuing futures in a number of different areas, most of which involve STEM. They are backed by a supportive school district and mentored by dedicated individuals who have a wide variety of careers.

We depend on sponsors and donors to help us purchase food for students, robot parts, team apparel, and event entry fees. Every dollar you donate gives a student an opportunity they can't get anywhere else! All funds go directly to the South San High School Robotics student activity account, and a full accounting of money used is available at any time upon request.
