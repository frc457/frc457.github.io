The Grease Monkeys

Mission

To inspire a passion for STEM in our community through outreach, competition, teamwork, and innovation, empowering students to grow and realize their full potential.

Vision

A team where every student, regardless of background, can discover a path into STEM and carry that confidence into whatever comes next.

Our History

The South San Antonio Robotics program was originally established in 1999 at South San Antonio High School in San Antonio, Texas. FRC Team 457 was founded by the Aerospace Engineering Club, which was made up of then-physics teacher Mike Henry, one mentor, and a group of ten highly motivated students.

In 2000, we were the first team in the San Antonio area to compete in the FIRST Robotics Competition. The name Team Gamma was officially adopted in 2001. The current team identity, the Grease Monkeys, was adopted in 2002, the same year that we won the Lone Star Regional.

What We Do

One of the goals of the robotics program has always been to help students increase their self-confidence by learning technical and non-technical skills. Members can look forward to an introduction to the worlds of engineering, manufacturing, and programming. They also develop valuable skills such as leadership, teambuilding, and communication. Mentors from different areas provide assistance and guidance while working with the team. We welcome all students who are interested.

The mission of our team is to inspire a passion for STEM in our community by providing a space for any student from any background to grow. Our team is a family, bringing together people with different strengths into one group to compete in a fun and engaging environment.
